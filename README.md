# Research Portfolio

Welcome to my animated research portfolio! Here, you will find an interactive showcase of my research work, complete with modern design elements and engaging animations.

## Sections

1. **Introduction**  
   - A brief overview of my academic background and research interests.
   
2. **Research Projects**  
   - Detailed descriptions of my research projects, methodologies, and findings.
   
3. **Publications**  
   - A list of my published papers, including links to the full texts.
   
4. **Presentations**  
   - Keynote presentations and talks I've given at conferences.
   
5. **Contact**  
   - How to reach me for collaborations or inquiries.

## Features

- **Interactive Icons**: Click on the icons to navigate through sections.
- **Animations**: Smooth transitions and hover effects to enhance user experience.
- **Modern Design**: A sleek layout that showcases content beautifully.
